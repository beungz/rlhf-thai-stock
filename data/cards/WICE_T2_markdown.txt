## WICE LOGISTICS PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (WICE)
- Market: SET | Industry: Services | Sector: Transportation & Logistics
- Business: The Company and its subsidiary are international logistics service and solution providers, including import and export by both sea freight (Full and Less Container Loading) and by air freight, custom clearance and land transportation which the company aims at fulfilling wide range of customer requirements. Inaddition, the company provides Door-to-Door delivery which is transferring goods from a factory to another and the exporter is responsible for freight charges. Also, the company provides Exwork delivery which is transferring goods from a factory to another and the importer is responsible for freight charges.
