## PRG CORPORATION PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (PRG)
- Market: SET | Industry: Agro & Food Industry | Sector: Food & Beverage
- Business: Patum Rice Mill and Granary Public Company Limited (PRG) is the producer and distributor of milled rice for the local and the international market. PRG's products are milled rice packed in plastic bag of 2 kgs. and 5 kgs. and in polypropylene bag of 15 kgs., 48 kgs., 49 kgs., and 50 kgs. PRG brands are 'Mah Boonkrong' , ' Mah Boonkrong Plus', and 'Jasmine Gold' PRG is also doing business of restaurant and food court.
