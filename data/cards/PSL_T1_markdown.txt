## PRECIOUS SHIPPING PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (PSL)
- Market: SET | Industry: Services | Sector: Transportation & Logistics
- Business: The Company is one of largest pure dry cargo ship-owning companies operating in the small handy size sector of the tramp freight market.
