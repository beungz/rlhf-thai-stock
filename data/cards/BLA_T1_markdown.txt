## BANGKOK LIFE ASSURANCE PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (BLA)
- Market: SET | Industry: Financials | Sector: Insurance
- Business: Bangkok Life Assurance Public Company Limited is a life insurance provider and a provider of comprehensive financial planning with a firm belief that life insurance is a way for an individual to mitigate risks from losses of life or organs, from becoming disabled, and from the lack of income in old age.
