## SINGER THAILAND PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (SINGER)
- Market: SET | Industry: Services | Sector: Commerce
- Business: Singer Thailand Public Company Limited (the 'Company') is the distributor of various products under the "Singer" brand, such as: sewing machines and various types of household electrical appliances. Additionally, the Company also distributes appliances and equipments for commercial use, such as: refrigerators, coolers, agricultural tools and equipment, mobile phone airtime vending machines, petrol vending machines, and slush ice machines; as well as acts a distributor of various brands of mobile hone products and accessories.
