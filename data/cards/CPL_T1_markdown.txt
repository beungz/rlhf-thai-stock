## CPL GROUP PUBLIC COMPANY LIMITED (CPL)
- Market: SET | Industry: Consumer Products | Sector: Fashion
- Business: The Company is a one of world class leather tannery. Products are cow finished leathers including cow split leathers as well. Product ranges from aniline leather, pigmented leather, oiled leather, waterproof leather and etc, thru to split suede and finished splits. Products are sold to global name brands such as Adidas, Timberland, Marks& Spencer and Puma and etc.
